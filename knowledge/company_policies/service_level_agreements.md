# Enterprise Service Level Agreements (SLA) Policy

## 1. Scope and Classification
This document governs SLA definitions, response targets, and escalation matrices for all contracted accounts.

### Severity Levels
- **Severity 1 (Critical Outage)**: Core transaction or ingestion path completely offline. Response target: < 15 mins for Platinum, < 1 hour for Gold.
- **Severity 2 (High Degradation)**: Major business feature impaired; workaround available. Response target: < 2 hours.
- **Severity 3 (Medium / Normal)**: Non-critical query degradation or admin portal issue. Response target: < 8 hours.
- **Severity 4 (Low / Advisory)**: General questions or cosmetic requests. Response target: Next business day.

## 2. Escalation Procedure
When an enterprise account triggers a Sev-1 incident, the Incident Commander must notify the Designated Technical Account Manager and Customer Success Director immediately.
